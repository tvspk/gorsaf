0846|interview (job interview; media interview)|I had an interview for the job yesterday (cyfweliad → [g]yfweliad as the object after ces i).|cyfweld (to interview) + -iad (the act): an interview held on ice skates in the middle of a frozen lake.
+pozn=Cyfweliad (40% o'r marciau) = the oral part of the exam (p. 265); not in the Geirfa; own example

0848|died|The actor John Jones died at his home (marw → [f]arw after bu; cartref → [g]artref after ei = his).|bu = was (past of bod) + marw → farw: 'was dead', i.e. died.

0849|century|The house was built in the twentieth century (canrif → [g]anrif after the ordinal number, feminine).|can (hundred) + rhif (number): a hundred candles blazing on one birthday cake.

0855|coming in (of news)|News is coming in of an accident on the M4 (damwain → [dd]amwain after am).|dod (to come) + i law (to hand): the news comes to hand.
